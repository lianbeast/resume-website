ABOUT

Building the Networks
That Connect Everything

From desktop support at Jorge Scientific to designing VoLTE/5G core signaling for T-Mobile, my career has been a steady climb through the layers of network infrastructure. I specialize in the intersection of telecom core networks and enterprise IT — a space where SS7/SIGTRAN meets Active Directory, and where SIP trunks coexist with SharePoint farms.

My approach is hands-on and methodical: map the call flow, trace the signaling, isolate the fault, apply the fix. Whether it's provisioning an eNodeB, configuring Nokia NetAct thresholds, or scripting AMOS batch operations, I bring both depth and breadth to every engagement.
